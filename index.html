About Activities

Welcome to my website! I am Maxence Larrieu and I have been working in scholarly communication and open science since 2015. My latest project is TSOSI, a data platform which aims to broaden financial support for open infrastructures. See more tsosi.org. Another project I previously led is OPUS, a diamond open access publishing platform running on PKP free softwares: opus.u-paris.fr.


I work at Grenoble Alpes University, at the GRICAD laboratory, with a focus on research data. In open science I am interested in policies, technology, and more broadly the ecosystem of scientific knowledge.
